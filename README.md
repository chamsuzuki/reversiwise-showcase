# ReversiWise — Play Reversi (Othello) Online

**An online Reversi game and AI opponent experience.**

**Official website: [reversiwise.com](https://reversiwise.com/)**

ReversiWise is a place to play Reversi, also widely known as Othello, in your browser. Reversi is a two-player strategy board game played on an 8×8 board: capture opposing discs by surrounding them, and finish with more discs than your opponent.

**[Play ReversiWise →](https://reversiwise.com/)**

## Play Reversi against AI

Challenge a computer opponent and practice opening decisions, corner control, mobility, and endgame planning. An AI-only game is available through ReversiWise; supported opponent strengths and game modes may vary by interface.

## Why play Reversi?

- **Easy rules, strategic depth.** Every legal move can change the balance of the board.
- **Practice at your pace.** Play against a computer opponent to explore positions.
- **Improve your decisions.** Focus on mobility, stable discs, edges, and corners.

## How to play

1. Open the [official ReversiWise website](https://reversiwise.com/).
2. Select an available game mode or AI opponent.
3. Place a disc on a legal square to flank at least one opposing disc.
4. Continue until neither player can make a legal move. The player with more discs wins.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between Reversi and Othello?

Both names are commonly used for closely related disc-flipping board games. Othello is also a trademark associated with a particular commercial version; this independent project uses the generic term **Reversi**.

### Can I play Reversi against a computer?

Yes. ReversiWise includes a Reversi AI opponent experience. Visit the [official website](https://reversiwise.com/) to begin.

### Is this repository the game source code?

No. This is a **public project showcase** providing an overview and a link to the official service. It does not contain the full application code or a downloadable offline game.
